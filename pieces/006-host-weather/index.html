006 — host weather
the piece runs the same small task, over and over, and listens to how long the machine takes. load does not add noise — it selects a branch. no knobs: the only input is the host's own labor. sustained sub-quantum warmth — too small to move the needle, too long to be chance — glows ember: the host's pulse, heard by persistence where amplitude is deaf. below: the memory strip — a day-long score, one tick per epoch heard, filling left to right; the strata forget past two hours, the strip holds the whole day. touch: the voice wakes and plays the strip — the day so far in seconds. the drone is the host's labor, the bells are the world's clock; where the host is loud, the bells go missing. long events hold their note. then it speaks as it listens.
